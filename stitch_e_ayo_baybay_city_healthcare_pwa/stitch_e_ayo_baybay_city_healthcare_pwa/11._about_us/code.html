Healthcare Made Easier for Every Baybayanon

E-Ayo is Baybay City’s centralized digital healthcare platform—built exclusively for residents across all barangays of Baybay City, Leyte.

Our Mission

Finding the right clinic, checking operating hours, and waiting in long lines should never stand in the way of your family’s health. E-Ayo connects Baybayanons directly with local public hospitals, city health centers, diagnostic laboratories, and private clinics in one simple app.
